How we work

Five steps, and you see working software at step three.

  1. Brief

    A call or message to understand the problem, the users and the deadline.

  2. Scope

    A written list of what is included, a fixed price or estimate, and a delivery date.

  3. Build

    Working versions shared early and often, so changes stay cheap.

  4. Ship

    Deployment, domain, monitoring and a handover of the code and accounts.

  5. Support

    Bug fixes and updates after launch, agreed up front.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

How much does a project cost?

After a free discovery call you get a fixed quote. The price depends on scope, not on hours.

Who owns the code?

You do. At delivery, the code, hosting and domain move to your accounts.

How long does it take?

Most websites take 2–4 weeks, web apps 4–8 weeks and custom software 6–12 weeks.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Bug fixes and updates are agreed up front, and a monthly plan is available.

How does a project run?

Brief, scope, build in short cycles, ship, support. You see working software early.

Can the website be in more than one language?

Yes. A site can be built in two or more languages when the project needs it.
